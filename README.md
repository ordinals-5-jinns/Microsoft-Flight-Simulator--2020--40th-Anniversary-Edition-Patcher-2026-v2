## 🎮 About the Game

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a flight simulation game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Xbox Game Studios. It launched for PC on August 18, 2020, and received the free 40th Anniversary Edition update on November 11, 2022. The simulator models aircraft systems, flight physics, worldwide scenery, flight planning, live weather, time of day, air traffic, and multiple aircraft types, including airplanes, helicopters, and gliders.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition**. This trainer provides optional assistance for Microsoft Flight Simulator by managing aircraft fuel, damage effects, simulation time, and flight positioning. It is intended for practice flights, sightseeing, difficult approaches, aircraft testing, and exploring the simulator without restarting after a crash or fuel shortage.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for flight-simulation newcomers, experienced pilots practicing approaches, creators recording cinematic flights, users testing aircraft and scenery, and players who want reduced frustration during long or demanding flights.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Microsoft Flight Simulator uses aircraft-specific flight controls rather than traditional character movement. Use the trainer's assistance options for positioning, slew movement, or simulation pauses, then return to normal flight controls for takeoff, navigation, approach, and landing practice. Helicopters, gliders, and fixed-wing aircraft require different control techniques.

### Tools & Inventory

The simulator has no conventional inventory system. Aircraft equipment, fuel quantity, payload, avionics, and configuration are selected through the aircraft interface, flight setup screens, or cockpit controls. The trainer focuses on live flight values rather than adding or changing an inventory.

### World & Environment

The game provides a worldwide environment with airports, terrain, cities, navigation data, changing time of day, live or preset weather, air traffic, flight plans, and activities. Use time assistance and damage protection to practice routes, explore scenery, or repeat challenging weather and landing conditions without restarting the flight.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no standard combat gameplay, weapon system, enemy roster, or combat progression in Microsoft Flight Simulator. Aircraft collisions and crashes are simulation events rather than combat encounters, and the trainer's protection options are intended to prevent the flight from ending after damage.
